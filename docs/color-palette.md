# Student colour palette

The site sits on a deep indigo night. Amber is the complement of that blue, so buttons and warm edges carry the energy. The seven topics share one saturation and step around the wheel, so each card is distinct without turning into neon.

Body text on the cream paper is `#2C1810` on `#FFF8EE`, about 16:1 (WCAG AAA). Topic names and the practice card use darker shades of the same hues so they stay at least 4.5:1.

## Ground and paper

| Role | Hex | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night indigo | `#12244F` | Ground behind the sky image. Hue about 226°. |
| Paper | `#FFF8EE` | Warm page, not grey. |
| Ink | `#2C1810` | Body text on paper. |
| Soft ink | `#5C4636` | Secondary text on paper, about 8:1. |
| Amber edge | `#F0B429` | Warm border, same family as the CTA. |

## Complementary call to action

## Topic hues

Same idea: saturated, similar lightness, different hue. Names on the cards use the darker column.

| Topic | Hue | Name colour on paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 音名 | violet | `#4C2DB5` |
| 調號 | blue | `#1D4ED8` |
| 唱名 | green | `#0B7A45` |
| 和弦 | magenta | `#A21CAF` |
| 節奏 | coral | `#C4233A` |
| 術語 | teal | `#0E7490` |
| 樂器 | gold | `#B45309` |
| 聽力 | quiet indigo | `#4A5278` |

練習模式 stays a cream card. Green `#1FCB6A` is the border and the top band, and the title is `#0B7A45`. 1分鐘挑戰 and 零失誤挑戰 use amber `#FFD15A`–`#FF9F1C` with `#3A2208` text when they are the chosen mode.

和弦 is magenta so it is not the same orange as the buttons. 樂器 is a brighter gold than the earlier brown. Both names clear 4.5:1 on the cream paper.

Answer colours inside a game (correct green, wrong red) stay the teaching cues they already were.
